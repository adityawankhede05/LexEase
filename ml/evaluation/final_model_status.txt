--- 2. RISK SCORE CALCULATION — EXACT METHODOLOGY ---

Step 1: Tokenize clause text with AnkushRaheja tokenizer (max_length=256, padding=True)
Step 2: Pass through BERT encoder → pooled [CLS] token representation (dim=768)
Step 3: Apply Dropout(0.25)
Step 4: Pass through regressor → raw_score = Sigmoid(Linear(384→1)(ReLU(Linear(768→384)(pooled)))) ∈ [0, 1]
Step 5: risk_score_pct = raw_score × 100  →  range [0, 100]

Step 6: THRESHOLD CLASSIFICATION (calibrated on validation.csv via grid search, then FROZEN):
  LOW    if risk_score_pct  < 41.00
  MEDIUM if 41.00 ≤ risk_score_pct < 60.00
  HIGH   if risk_score_pct ≥ 60.00

The thresholds were selected to maximise Macro F1 on validation.csv only.
The 150 real-world labels were NOT used to choose thresholds (leakage-safe).

--- 6. CLAUSE CLASSIFIER: WHY IT RETURNS UNKNOWN/OTHER FOR INDIAN CLAUSES ---

The Experiment 8 model was trained on 22 classes and achieves 81.25% on its OWN test split.
However, in this integration test, ALL 15 clauses returned UNKNOWN/OTHER.

Root cause: The confidence threshold in the integration test is 0.40.
The model outputs softmax probabilities across 22 classes.
For short, generic clause text it produces a spread-out distribution with no class > 0.40.
Lowering the confidence threshold to 0.25 would surface the top prediction, but with lower reliability.

This is NOT a bug in the model weights — it is a threshold tuning issue in the integration layer.
The model's top-1 predictions do cover Rental, NDA etc. at lower confidence levels.


--- 7. KNOWN LIMITATIONS ---

1. Clause Classification Confidence Threshold Too High
   The 0.40 confidence cutoff is too conservative for the 22-class Experiment 8 model.
   Recommendation: Lower to 0.25–0.30 for production, or report top-1 with confidence < 0.40
   as a soft warning rather than UNKNOWN/OTHER.

2. Risk Model Cannot Separate Contextual LOW vs HIGH for Same Clause Type
   The integration test shows that LOW vs HIGH variants of the same clause type
   (e.g. NDA Confidentiality: 58.32 vs 58.36) receive identical scores.
   This is the core contextual risk limitation.
   Root cause: The training data does not contain enough contrastive examples for
   Indian-domain clauses. The 240 synthetic contrastive examples (5 domains × 3 levels × 16)
   are too sparse to teach the regression head distinct contextual patterns.

3. Loan / Finance Domain — No Training Data
   The risk model has no loan/finance clause training examples.
   The Loan — Penalty (HIGH) clause scored only 50.55 (MEDIUM).
   Do NOT claim loan-domain risk generalizes.

4. The best standalone risk accuracy remains 58.67% (Pretrained OOF Baseline AnkushRaheja).
   The fine-tuned final_risk_model achieves 37.33% overall accuracy and 96.30% HIGH recall.
   These are different trade-offs. For a safety-first system, 6A / final_risk_model is preferable.
   For highest overall accuracy, the Pretrained OOF Baseline is preferable.

5. Experiment 8 corporate clause accuracy dropped from 95.90% (Exp 3) to 41.38%.
   The corporate classifier must remain Experiment 3 for corporate documents.
