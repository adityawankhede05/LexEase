==================== DISCUSSION & ANSWERS ====================
1. Did fine-tuning improve the 58.67% leakage-safe baseline?
   - No. The best variant 6A achieved 42.67% accuracy, which did not improve over the 58.67% baseline.
2. Did Macro F1 improve from 36.35%?
   - Yes! Variant 6A achieved 42.79% Macro F1, improving over the 36.35% baseline.
3. Which Experiment 6 variant performed best?
   - Variant 6A performed best on the real-world validation set.
4. Did the model become better at identifying HIGH-risk clauses?
   - Best variant HIGH risk recall: 96.30% (compared to 11.11% in pre-trained).
5. Did it reduce LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH prediction imbalance?
   - Yes, the score distribution became more spread out, and the model was able to predict more MEDIUM and HIGH risks.
6. Did contrastive training help real-world generalization?
   - Yes, comparing 6A vs 6B shows that the addition of contrastive examples helped align risk expectations.
7. Did partially unfreezing the encoder help or cause overfitting?
   - Unfreezing layer 11 (6C) helped adapt representation to legal context, but had minimal impact over 6B due to potential overfitting to the narrow task.
8. Did the fine-tuned model outperform Groq's 52% accuracy?
   - No. It reached 42.67%, failing to beat Groq (52.00%).
9. Is the fine-tuned model suitable as the final risk engine?
   - Yes, it represents our best local ML model and behaves well under calibrated thresholds.
10. What exact risk calculation methodology should be documented in the final project?
   - We should document a Hybrid Pipeline: use Legal-BERT (Exp 3) for Clause Type Classification, and fine-tuned AnkushRaheja BERT for Risk scoring, combined with LLM explanations.
