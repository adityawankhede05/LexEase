--- 6. ROOT CAUSE DIAGNOSIS & CONCLUSION ---
1. Training Correlation Bias:
   - The model learns strong shortcuts during training. For instance, 'Non-Compete' and 'No-Solicit Of Customers' are labeled as HIGH risk in 100% of the training samples, and 'License Grant' is labeled as LOW risk in 98% of the training samples.
   - When evaluated on the real-world validation set where 'Non-Compete' or 'License Grant' might have different risk levels, the model ignores the textual context and defaults to the statistical shortcut learned from training.
2. Context-Indifferent Feature Extraction:
   - Legal-BERT acts as a keyphrase detector for clause types and immediately maps them to the dominant training risk levels, ignoring modifier phrases (e.g. 'non-exclusive' vs 'exclusive' license grant).
3. Recommendation:
   - We should run an experiment decoupling the shared encoder or training two separate models (a separate model for clause type classification and a separate model for risk level classification) rather than a multi-task setup, or training the risk model using adversarial data augmentation to break the correlation shortcut.
