--- DETAILED FINDINGS PER DATASET ---

1. ml/data/splits/train.csv  (7,557 rows — REAL)
   - 41 unique clause types, all from corporate/commercial US-style legal contracts.
   - Only 9 clause types appear with LOW + MEDIUM + HIGH examples simultaneously.
   - Most clause types are heavily skewed to one risk level:
       Document Name: LOW=123, HIGH=9, MED=2   → 94% of examples are LOW
       Governing Law: LOW=304, HIGH=43, MED=2  → 86% examples are LOW
       Expiration Date: HIGH=210, LOW=114, MED=8 → MEDIUM almost absent
   - Zero Indian-domain clause types (Rent, Security Deposit, Non-Compete etc.)
   - Labels: REAL (from CUAD dataset annotations)
   - Contextual risk variation present? PARTIALLY — 9/41 clause types have ≥3 examples
     at each risk level, but many are still dominated by one class.

2. ml/data/splits/validation.csv  (945 rows — REAL)
   - Same corporate domain as train.csv. Mirrored distribution.
   - Used for threshold calibration. Must remain untouched for evaluation.

3. ml/data/experiment5_contrastive_train.csv  (72 rows — SYNTHETIC)
   - 16 corporate clause types (License Grant, Exclusivity, Cap On Liability etc.)
   - Each type has LOW + MEDIUM + HIGH examples — good contrastive structure.
   - ALL 72 are marked source=synthetic_contrastive.
   - No Indian-domain clause types.
   - Too small to generalise across domains, but valuable for corporate contextual risk.

4. ml/data/final_risk_dataset.csv  (1,362 rows — MIXED)
   - 1,050 rows REAL (sampled from train.csv corporate splits, balanced)
   - 312 rows SYNTHETIC (Indian domains + Exp5 contrastive)
   - Indian-domain breakdown: Rental=96, Employment=48, Loan=48, NDA=48
   - ALL 240 Indian-domain rows are 100% SYNTHETIC (auto-generated text variations)
   - Only 5 Indian clause types have multi-risk examples:
       Confidentiality, Interest & Penalties, Non-Compete, Security Deposit, Termination
   - CRITICAL: The Indian examples are variations of the same 3 template sentences
     with random number substitution (e.g. "30 days" → "45 days").
     They do NOT represent genuine contextual wording differences across risk levels.
   - This is why the integration test showed NDA Confidentiality LOW and HIGH
     both scoring ~58.3 — the model cannot distinguish them.

5. ml/data/real_world_validation/validation_clauses.csv  (150 rows — REAL)
   - 5 document types: Commercial Lease (30), Employment (30), NDA (30),
     SaaS (30), Software Dev (30)
   - Human-labelled risk: LOW=87, MEDIUM=36, HIGH=27
   - NOTE: The Commercial Lease (rental) domain risk distribution is:
       LOW=27, MEDIUM=2, HIGH=1  (i.e. 90% of real rental clauses are LOW risk)
   - Employment domain: HIGH=23, LOW=5, MEDIUM=2 (HIGH dominated)
   - NDA domain: MED=23, LOW=6, HIGH=1 (MEDIUM dominated)
   - This is the ONLY held-out real-world test set. MUST NOT be used for training.

6. ml/data/experiment8_indian_clause_dataset.csv  (1,300 rows)
   - Contains ONLY clause type labels. NO risk_level column.
   - CANNOT be used for risk model training.


--- CONTEXTUAL RISK LEARNING ASSESSMENT ---

For a model to learn that the SAME clause type can be LOW or HIGH depending on wording,
it needs genuine examples where:
  - Text A (same clause type) → LOW risk (because: limited/mutual/capped)
  - Text B (same clause type) → HIGH risk (because: unlimited/unilateral/perpetual)

CORPORATE DOMAIN:
  - train.csv: 9 clause types have all 3 risk levels, but LOW is over-represented.
  - Experiment 5 contrastive: 16 clause types with genuine LOW/MED/HIGH variation.
  - Assessment: ADEQUATE for corporate contextual risk. The AnkushRaheja pretrained
    model already learned this from its original 7,500-example training, confirmed
    by its 58.67% leakage-safe real-world accuracy.

INDIAN LEGAL DOMAINS:
  - Rental / Lease:    96 rows, ALL synthetic text-number variations. No genuine
                       wording diversity. Risk scores are indistinguishable.
  - Employment:         48 rows, ALL synthetic. Non-compete has multi-risk examples
                       but these are trivial rewrites (3 km → 5 km radius).
  - NDA:               48 rows, ALL synthetic. Integration test showed 0.04-point
                       score difference between LOW and HIGH variants → effectively
                       no contextual separation.
  - Loan / Finance:     48 rows, ALL synthetic. Model scored HIGH penalty clause as
                       MEDIUM in integration test.
  - Property:           0 rows with risk labels.
  - Service:            0 rows with risk labels.
  - Partnership:        0 rows with risk labels.
  - General Indian:     0 rows with risk labels.


--- DECISION ---

B) INSUFFICIENT REAL DATA

The available data is NOT sufficient to train a defensible multi-domain Indian legal
risk model that demonstrates contextual risk learning across Indian legal domains.


--- EXACT MISSING DATA REQUIRED ---

For the claim of Indian multi-domain contextual risk to be defensible, the following
REAL (non-synthetic) labelled data is required:

  1. Rental / Lease domain:
     Minimum: 150 real clause snippets with genuine risk variation.
     Required: Genuine LOW vs HIGH examples with wording differences
     (e.g. refundable vs non-refundable deposit; reasonable vs excessive forfeiture).

  2. Employment domain:
     Minimum: 150 real clause snippets.
     Required: Genuine examples where the same clause type (Non-Compete, Termination,
     Confidentiality) has different wording at LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH risk levels.

  3. NDA / Confidentiality domain:
     Minimum: 100 real clause snippets.
     Required: Genuine perpetual vs time-limited, unilateral vs mutual examples
     with human-verified LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH labels.

  4. Loan / Finance domain:
     Minimum: 100 real clause snippets.
     Required: Standard interest vs compound-penalty vs seizure-of-assets wording.

  5. Property / Sale:
     Minimum: 100 real clause snippets.
     Required: Standard registration vs broad indemnity vs unlimited liability.

  Source: Real Indian legal agreements (rental deeds, employment contracts, loan
  agreements, sale deeds, NDAs from Indian jurisdictions) — NOT synthetic rewriting.

  Total minimum requirement: ~600 real Indian-domain risk-labelled clauses.
  Current real Indian-domain data: 0 rows.


--- WHAT CAN BE DONE RIGHT NOW (WITHOUT NEW DATA) ---

The current pretrained AnkushRaheja model (ml/models/final_risk_model/) remains the
best available local risk model, with:
  - 58.67% leakage-safe accuracy on the 150-clause real-world test set
  - Adequate corporate domain contextual risk learning
  - Partial Indian domain risk detection (Indemnity=HIGH, Governing Law=LOW correct;
    but NDA, Loan, Salary levels are not well separated)

For the final-year project presentation/viva, the honest documentation is:
  "The system uses a Legal-BERT-based risk regression model achieving 58.67% leakage-safe
  accuracy on real-world corporate legal clauses. Indian-domain risk assessment is partial
  and is augmented by routing ambiguous clauses to an LLM (Groq API) for contextual
  validation. Full Indian-domain ML risk learning requires ~600 additional real labelled
  examples that are outside the scope of this project."
